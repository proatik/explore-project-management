🎯 Key takeaways

  • A TPM's job is decisions with the trade-offs made visible: scope, time, cost, risk.
  • Report the truth early: ranges, earned value and an honest RAG.
  • Close the loop: success criteria at the start, sign-off and a retro at the end.
