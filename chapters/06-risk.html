Name it, then size it

Five ways to respond

Risk isn't only bad news. PMBOK 7 manages threats and opportunities.

How much buffer?

Total EMV → suggested contingency

Illustrative numbers. Threats are negative, opportunities positive.

Where the reserve lives
🧯 Contingency reserve

For identified risks (known-unknowns). Part of the cost baseline. Usually the project team manages it.

🏦 Management reserve

For unidentified risks (unknown-unknowns). Outside the baseline. Management approves its use.

🎯 Key takeaways

  • A risk is uncertain and in the future. An issue is happening now.
  • Every risk needs an owner, a response and a trigger.
  • Manage opportunities too. Risk isn't only bad news.
