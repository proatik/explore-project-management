The scope statement

One page everyone signs. "Out of scope" matters as much as "in".

✅ In scope
  • Login, incl. SSO
  • Order history (last 3 years)
  • Raise and track support tickets
  • Web, responsive to tablet
⛔ Out of scope
  • Native mobile apps
  • Live chat
  • Payments and refunds
  • Languages other than English
🎯 Acceptance criteria
  • Pages load in under 2s (p95)
  • WCAG 2.2 AA
  • Sponsor sign-off after UAT
  • Support team trained

Also listed: assumptions, constraints, deliverables. Together with the WBS and WBS dictionary this becomes the scope baseline.

    Scope creep vs gold plating

    Both skip change control. They differ in who adds the work.

    🐛

    Scope creep

    • Additions requested by customers or stakeholders
    • Accepted informally, no impact analysis
    • Often starts with vague requirements
    • Fix: clear scope + change control
    ✨

    Gold plating

    • Extras the team adds that nobody asked for
    • Well meant: "users will love it"
    • Costs time, adds risk and untested code
    • Fix: build to acceptance criteria, propose extras as changes

    🎯 Key takeaways

    • Scope is written and agreed: what's in, what's out, and how we know it's done.
    • Decompose deliverables (WBS or backlog) until each piece is estimable and owned.
    • Every change gets an impact analysis and a decision. Silent yeses become overruns.
