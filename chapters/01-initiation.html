Business case

Why should we invest?

  • Owned by the sponsor
  • Justifies the investment against options
  • PRINCE2: kept up to date and re-checked at each stage
Project charter

How do we start, and who decides?

  • Issued by the sponsor, drafted with the PM
  • Formally authorizes the project and the PM's authority (PMBOK)
  • PRINCE2 has no charter: the Project Brief, then the Project Initiation Documentation (PID), fill that role

🎯 Key takeaways

  • The business case says why; the charter authorizes the project and the PM.
  • Write SMART objectives and success criteria before work starts.
  • Know who decides and who can block on day one.
