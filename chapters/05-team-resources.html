The cost of switching

Projects at the same time
  • Each switch costs time to reload context.
  • Finish one thing before starting the next.
  • Shared people are a schedule risk (see Ch 04).
Source: Gerald Weinberg, Quality Software Management (1992). A rule of thumb, not a measurement.

🎯 Key takeaways

  • Every task needs one Accountable person and at least one Responsible.
  • Plan with real capacity: subtract time off, meetings, on-call and switching.
  • Teams move through stages. Psychological safety is the foundation.
