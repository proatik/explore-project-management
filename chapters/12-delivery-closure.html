🚀 Launch

Decide, release, hand over

Agree the go/no-go criteria before the meeting, not during it.

🛑 Blocker

  • Stops the launch on its own
  • Open Sev-1 / Sev-2 defects
  • No tested rollback
  • Legal or security not approved

⚠️ Condition

  • Launch is OK if it's fixed by a date
  • Support training finishes day 2
  • Help-center article by end of week
  • Needs an owner and a deadline

🟢 Noise

  • Doesn't affect the decision
  • Cosmetic Sev-4 bugs
  • "Nice to have" polish
  • Logged to the backlog

PRINCE2 · Closing a Project

  1. Prepare planned closure: confirm the products were accepted
  2. Hand over products to operations and support
  3. Evaluate the project: End Project Report and Lessons Report
  4. Recommend closure to the Project Board

Benefits usually arrive after closure, so the Benefits Management Approach hands their review to the business.

Retrospective vs lessons learned

Same idea, different audience. Pick a retro format to see a sample board.

🔁 Retrospective

  • For: the team
  • When: every sprint or milestone
  • Goal: improve how we work now
  • Output: 1–3 actions with owners

🏛️ Lessons learned

  • For: the organization
  • When: throughout, and at phase or project end
  • Goal: help future projects
  • Output: lessons register or knowledge base
The Prime Directive
"Regardless of what we discover, we understand and truly believe that everyone did the best job they could, given what they knew at the time, their skills and abilities, the resources available, and the situation at hand."
Norm Kerth, Project Retrospectives (2001). Read it at the start of every retro.

Benefits realization

You deliver outputs. The business wanted outcomes. Tap a point after launch.

📦 Output

  • What the project delivers
  • "A self-service password reset"
  • Done at launch

📈 Outcome

  • The change it causes
  • "Password tickets down 30% in 6 months"
  • Measured weeks or months later

    🎯 Key takeaways

    • Go/no-go is a decision with criteria: blockers stop a launch, conditions need an owner and a date.
    • Done means accepted, handed over and closed, not just deployed.
    • Measure outcomes after launch. Retros improve the team, lessons improve the organization.
