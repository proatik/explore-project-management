    Positions hide interests

    From Fisher & Ury, Getting to Yes. Tap a demand to see what's really behind it.

    The 4 principles
    • Separate the people from the problem
    • Focus on interests, not positions
    • Invent options for mutual gain
    • Insist on objective criteria
    Know your BATNA
    • Best Alternative To a Negotiated Agreement
    • What you'll do if there's no deal
    • E.g. "No SSO by June → we launch without it and give Acme a dated roadmap commitment."
    • A strong BATNA lets you stay calm and say no to a bad deal

    🎯 Key takeaways

    • Never a flat yes or no: offer options with their cost.
